Allow me to clap for you amidst this sea of men. I will sit patiently and admire your performance. I watch as your crooked extremities shuffle back and forth. I study the caricature of your head’s constant hum. You are springing up and down, like a stupid dog whose spirit runs wild and free. I look at you and every neuron in my brain writes to tell me that you, whose shadows linger in the doorway, are beautiful.

I know you, too, will learn how to surrender, drown in song, and unravel your arms. Let them loose, child. I say this when I sink my knees onto the dirt. If I pray long enough to the moon, will she whisper back and teach me why you defy against surrender? Will she tell me why my fingers strain reaching out for your tenses?

I sit in silence because those red lights home can easily blur my eyes. When you get back, let me know. Will you have enough strength to open the door? I know that you are so strong, and so very gentle.

I’ve been writing to you like a mad-man.

I hope your heart rests well, and it is lighter than mine. I hope you ease your mind, and you run in peace. Good night, you silly, silly, boy. I pray to inch no closer to this depraved cruelty and desire that makes me want to anchor in your skin.

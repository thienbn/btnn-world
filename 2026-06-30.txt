Allow me to clap for you amidst sea of men. I will sit patiently and admire your performance. I watch as your crooked extremities shuffle back and forth. Your body caricatures the constant hum of fire and earthquakes inside you. I gaze as you spring up and down, like a stupid dog whose spirit runs wild and free. Still, every neuron in my brain is writing to tell me that your chaos is beautiful.

I know you, too, will learn how to surrender, drown in this song, and unravel your arms. Let them loose, child. Let’s get cozy and dig our toes deeper. I say this to you while I sink to my knees on this dirt. If I pray enough to the moon, will she whisper to teach me all about your great act of defiance against surrender? Will she tell me why my fingers strain reaching for your tenses? You think your light dims more than mine.

I sit in silence because those red lights home easily blur my eyes. When you get back, please let me know. Will you have enough strength to open the door? Yes, I know your body is healthy.

I’ve been writing to you like a mad-man.

I hope your heart rests well, and that it is lighter than mine. I hope you ease your mind, and that you run in peace. Silly, silly, boy, you. I pray to inch no closer to depraved cruelty and desire. I pray to anchor in your skin. I pray to let us diverge, over and over again, and still find ways to meet.
